# Live Tape — Project Charter

**Status:** Active  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-26

---

## 1. Mission

Live Tape seeks to create transparent and executable market
infrastructure for live-event admission.

The underlying thesis is that secondary live-event admission is often
fragmented across ticket issuers, resale marketplaces, social groups,
and informal person-to-person transactions.

Live Tape seeks to improve this through:

- visible demand;
- visible supply;
- price discovery;
- standardized admission instruments;
- reliable transaction execution;
- settlement infrastructure;
- historical market data.

---

## 2. Initial Market

The initial research and validation wedge is:

**NYC nightlife, particularly transferable general-admission inventory.**

Reasons include:

- comparatively fungible admission rights;
- dense event communities;
- frequent events;
- constrained venue supply;
- existing informal resale behavior;
- short time-to-event horizons;
- potentially accessible independent promoters.

---

## 3. Current Product Thesis

The initial consumer-market thesis is:

A buyer should be able to express:

> "I will pay $X for this admission right."

A seller should be able to see executable demand and choose whether to
sell into it.

The project is testing whether:

1. people will place bids rather than only browse listings;
2. visible bids will cause ticket holders to sell;
3. concentrated liquidity creates meaningful price discovery;
4. tickets can settle reliably enough for users to trust the market.

These remain hypotheses until validated.

---

## 4. Longer-Term Direction

Possible expansion path:

NYC nightlife  
→ festivals  
→ concerts  
→ sports  
→ other live-event markets.

This expansion is not assumed to be inevitable.

Each market has different:

- fungibility;
- settlement;
- ticketing systems;
- liquidity;
- regulation;
- market structure.

---

## 5. Long-Term Infrastructure Thesis

If validated, Live Tape may evolve beyond a consumer resale marketplace
toward infrastructure involving:

- standardized ticket instruments;
- executable bid/ask markets;
- inventory provenance;
- settlement orchestration;
- market-data infrastructure;
- professional seller liquidity;
- promoter integrations;
- issuer integrations;
- potentially native custody/reissuance.

---

## 6. Non-Goals

Live Tape is not currently:

- a prediction market;
- a futures market;
- a derivatives exchange;
- a crypto/tokenization project;
- a ticket-investment product;
- an automated primary-ticket purchasing bot;
- an HFT system.

Tickets represent admission rights, not speculative financial contracts.

---

## 7. Operating Principle

Build software that helps Live Tape learn faster before building
software whose value assumes the hypotheses are already true.
